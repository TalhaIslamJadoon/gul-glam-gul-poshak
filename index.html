About Us

A small Abbottabad shop with a big love for wedding days

Gul & Glam — also known as Gul Poshak — began with a simple thought: every bride in Hazara deserves to wear the dress of her dreams, even if she only needs it for one magical day.

From our shop in Abbottabad, and for our brides in Mansehra, we keep a carefully chosen rental wardrobe of mehndi, barat, walima and formal outfits — each piece hand-picked for its workmanship, cared for like our own, and priced so the celebration never has to compromise.
